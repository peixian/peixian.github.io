    pytorch/torchtext incompatibility issues [4a1bd41a]

    Tags: pytorch, torchtext

    Torchtext has a few incompatibilites:

    • torchtext.data.dataset and torchtext.data.iterator are wrappers around pytorch.utils.data.Dataset and pytorch.utils.DataLoader

    • notably the torchtext.data.iterator is not compatible with the pytorch subset or the torch.utils.data.sampler

      • pytorch.org/docs/stable/data.html
      • pytorch.org/docs/stable/data.html#iterable-style-datasets
    • highlighted in: github.com/pytorch/text/issues/664

    • possible to get discuss.pytorch.org/t/out-of-vocabulary-keyerror-on-vocab-stoi-in-torchtext/65910

      • or github.com/pytorch/text/issues/618
      • or discuss.pytorch.org/t/typeerror-dataloader-object-is-not-callable/74979
