    5. Becoming an EMS, Urban and Wilderness

    1. Get general CPR and training

      • Fufilled by www.nols.edu/en/coursefinder/courses/wilderness-first-responder-WFR/

    2. Take EMT training, get certified by EMT program

      • Fufilled by www.nols.edu/en/coursefinder/courses/wilderness-emt-WEMT/

    3. Take NREMT exam

      • www.nremt.org/rwd/public
      • Also fufilled by WEMT training

    4. After passing the NREMT exam, must register with the state

      • Pick a specific county to operate in
    5. Look for jobs/volunteering
