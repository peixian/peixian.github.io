    iran's kidney market [1cfad4bd]

    Tags: iran, economics

    Iran has the world's only regulated kidney market

    • www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/pmc/articles/PMC2322914/
    • demand for organs has always exceeded supply, especially due to diabetes and hypertension
    • already appears to be booming in turkey, russia, and south africa
    • Iran used to pay for its citizens to have transplants (mostly in united kingdom), but the costs balloooned for people with end stage renal diease
    • illegal for anyone to have "middle men" in iran
    • anecdotal stories of people "touring" their kidneys in iran
    • currently (july 22nd, 2021) sells for 4600
